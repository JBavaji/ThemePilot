# Design System: ThemePilot
**Project ID:** 6483655198524984692

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
ThemePilot embodies an ultra-refined, high-precision Swiss-modernist developer tool aesthetic ("The Architectural Compiler"). The visual mood is dark matte, purposeful, and mathematically calibrated. The interface uses tonal contrast rather than high-contrast stroke dividers (the "No-Line Rule"), paired with subtle glassmorphic surface elevations and luminous chromatic conduit energy radiating from Electric Aero Blue to Iris Violet along active pipelines.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
* **Scaffold Obsidian Navy (`#070E17`):** Deepest structural base for canvas backgrounds and viewport framing.
* **Midnight Navy Slate (`#0D1D2E`):** Primary canvas surface background for main workbench zones and the hero backdrop.
* **Tailored Surface Slate (`#16283B`):** Secondary container surface for cards, preview panes, code editors, and floating panels.
* **Surface Container High (`#1E354D`):** Elevated interactive tiles, active tabs, and focused inputs.
* **Structural Steel Facet (`#2A4E6C`):** Subdued geometric facets, subtle hairline dividers, and muted bracket anchors.
* **Electric Aero Blue (`#38BDF8`):** Primary brand accent, glowing status conduits, focused borders, and token compiler indicators.
* **Iris Violet (`#818CF8`):** Secondary gradient transition partner, representing transpilation progression into multi-platform targets.
* **Chalk White (`#FFFFFF`):** Primary display typography, structural plates, and high-emphasis labels.
* **Muted Aero Slate (`#94A3B8`):** Secondary body text, inactive tab headers, and metadata captions.
* **Success Emerald (`#10B981` / `#062D1F`):** Compilation success indicator and zero-drift pass badges.

## 3. Typography Rules
* **Display / Headline:** `Space Grotesk` (Weights: 600, 700). Tightly tracked (`-0.02em`), geometric, technical confidence.
* **Body / Interface:** `Inter` (Weights: 400, 500, 600). Neutral, ultra-legible, balanced line-height (1.5).
* **Code / Telemetry:** `JetBrains Mono` or `Fira Code` (Weight: 400, 500). Monospace alignment for tokens, hex codes, and Dart/Tailwind/Swift/Compose snippets.

## 4. Component Stylings
* **Buttons:** Fully pill-shaped (`9999px` radius). Primary button features an Electric Aero Blue (`#38BDF8`) to Iris Violet (`#818CF8`) gradient with dark navy text (`#070E17`) and font-weight 600. Secondary buttons feature glassmorphic dark container styling (`#16283B`) with white text and smooth hover glow.
* **Cards & Panes:** Subtly rounded corners (`12px` to `16px`), matte dark surface (`#16283B`), whisper-soft diffused ambient shadow (`0 8px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`). Borderless or framed with whisper-thin `#1E354D` border.
* **Inputs & Omni-Bar:** Large pill or rounded container (`12px`–`9999px`) with dark inner surface (`#070E17`), subtle inner glow, placeholder text in `#94A3B8`, and crisp Aero Blue focus ring.
* **Badges & Chips:** Compact pill badges (`9999px`) with dark tinted surface and glowing colored dot or text.

## 5. Layout Principles
* **Rhythm:** Strict 8dp grid system (`8px`, `16px`, `24px`, `32px`, `48px`, `64px`).
* **Desktop Workbench Composition:** 1440px wide balanced split workbench. Centered hero zone with high-prominence omni-search input, leading seamlessly into an expansive dual-pane split workspace (Blueprint Silhouette Preview on Left, Tabbed Code Inspector on Right).
